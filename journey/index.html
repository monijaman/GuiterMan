03 / STRUMMING

Count out loud. Your hand keeps moving down on the numbers and up on the "&", even when it misses the strings.

04 / PICKING & FINGERSTYLE

Hold the chord shape and pick single strings. p = thumb (bass strings), i m a = index, middle, ring. With a pick, use down-strokes on the numbers and up-strokes on the "&".

An arpeggio is a chord played one note at a time. Over each chord, its own notes are the safest places to land, and they sit inside the scale position you just learned.
