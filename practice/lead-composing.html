Morning Light

C major · 72 BPM · gentle pop

Backing chords: C – G – Am – F. One chord per bar.

Why this lead works

A rising three-note idea opens the phrase. Bar 2 answers it lower; the final A is the third of F, making a warm ending. For a full tonic ending, follow with C and hold E or C.

The opening rest gives the line room to breathe. A–C–E outlines Am; D–B–G outlines G. The answer descends through F chord tones and finally returns to A. Add gentle vibrato to the final note after learning the rhythm.

Window Waltz

C major · 90 BPM · lyrical 3/4

Backing chords: C – Am – F – G – C – Am – G – C. One chord per bar.

Why this lead works

Three beats per bar create a lilting pulse. The first four bars ask a question on D over G; the next four reuse the opening and finish on C. Let the two-beat notes sing instead of rushing to the next bar.

Blue Lantern

A blues · 84 BPM · straight-eighth blues phrase

Backing chords: A7 – D7 – E7 – A7. One chord per bar.

Why this lead works

C gives a blues colour against A7’s C♯. Over D7, F♯ states the major third, while C adds the seventh. G♯ over E7 points strongly back to A. The written sharps are local accidentals; count these eighth notes evenly.
