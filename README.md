# Guitar Field Notes

Guitar Field Notes is a visual, browser-based guide to guitar fundamentals, fretboard knowledge, harmony, and songwriting practice.

## Open the guide

No installation, server, database, or build step is required. Open [index.html](index.html) directly in a browser. All interactive features run locally in the page.

## What is included

- **Course path:** Nine grade pages, from Beginner Grade 1 through Advanced Grade 9. Each grade contains four focused study units, explanations, and practical exercises.
- **Chord library:** [chords/index.html](chords/index.html) contains 204 chords across 12 roots and 17 qualities. A chord detail page includes its notes, interval formula, up to six playable shapes, note names on every dot, a 12-fret map, related keys, and example progressions.
- **Scales & Harmony:** [scales/index.html](scales/index.html) covers the fretboard map, octave shapes, a note-finder quiz, 12 scales in any key, the chords built from each scale, common progressions, chord-tone targets, playable lead licks, and a five-part song workshop.
- **Arpeggios:** [arpeggios/index.html](arpeggios/index.html) explains the difference between chords, arpeggios, and scales; demonstrates movable shapes and six picking patterns; and includes song-style examples and chord-change soloing practice.
- **Music theory:** [theory/index.html](theory/index.html) explains half and whole steps, the major and natural minor scales, the chord numbering system for every major and minor key, the circle of fifths, and bass notes of open chords. It then teaches how to link chords with scale and chromatic bass walks: a playable walk library, rhythm patterns, a fully linked I–vi–IV–V progression, and a walk builder for any two chords in a key.
- **Ear training:** [ear/index.html](ear/index.html) teaches relative pitch: the open strings as anchor notes, the feeling and resolution of every scale degree, all twelve intervals with song hooks and guitar shapes, and ten chord qualities. A training gym has seven drills (higher/lower, scale degrees, intervals, chord quality, name the note, find it on the neck, and play it back with the microphone), each with levels, instant feedback and saved scores. A microphone note detector names any note you play and shows where it lives on the neck.
- **Journey:** [journey/index.html](journey/index.html) asks four questions (mood, genre, tempo, level) and builds a complete plan: a key and mode that fit the mood, a progression with chord shapes for your level and alternatives, a strumming and a picking pattern for the genre, the solo scale with where to play it on the neck (every position shown), arpeggios for each chord inside that position, a generated lead line, and a step-by-step roadmap. Answers are kept in the URL, so a journey can be bookmarked or shared.
- **Practice:** [practice/index.html](practice/index.html) is a composition workshop for motifs, question-and-answer phrases, chord-tone melodies, rhythm, 12-bar blues soloing, intro hooks, vocal fills, endings, and an interactive sketchpad.

The home page also includes an interactive 12-fret scale map, chord progressions, picking examples, a metronome, practice tracking, and song starters.

## Notes about saved progress

Practice checklists and sketches are saved in the browser's local storage on the current device. Clearing site data or switching browsers can remove that saved progress.

Reference images are stored in `../resourcess/gmajor/`.
