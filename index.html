SET UP TO PLAY01—03
01

Parts, in a glance

Headstock holds the tuners. The neck carries the frets; the body amplifies the strings. Press just behind a fret wire.

02

Tune before you play

Use a clip-on or phone tuner. Turn the peg slowly until each open string reads E–A–D–G–B–E.

03

Hold it lightly

Sit tall, rest the waist on your leg, and bring the neck slightly upward. Keep your fretting thumb relaxed behind the neck.

!

Clean note check: fret close to the wire, use your fingertip, then pluck each string one at a time. A buzz usually means adjust your finger, not squeeze harder.

04 / CHORD PROGRESSIONS

Chords have a home key.

Build chords by stacking every other note of a scale. The key predicts which chords naturally belong together.

THE KEY BUILDERTRY A KEY

Triad recipe: 1 – 3 – 5. Stack every other scale note. C–E–G makes C major; flatten the 3rd (C–E♭–G) and it becomes C minor.

Major-key pattern: I · ii · iii · IV · V · vi · vii°
Uppercase is major, lowercase is minor; ° means diminished.

The C♯ in A7 is outside D natural minor. It pulls a half-step up to D, making the return to Dm feel resolved.

Try D harmonic minor over A7: D · E · F · G · A · B♭ · C♯. The chord tones of each chord are your safest landing notes.
